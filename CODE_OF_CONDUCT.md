# Una Labs community standards

We expect respectful, professional, inclusive collaboration.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, consent, and intellectual property;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, and deceptive behaviour;
- report security concerns privately rather than exploiting or publicizing them;
- follow maintainer instructions on scope and safe testing.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when these standards are breached. Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@unalabs.cloud`.
